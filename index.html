03 / 11

And businesses are drowning in it.

Not because the data is wrong. Because nobody taught them how to read it.

They built dashboards. They hired analysts. They bought tools. They generated reports.

04 / 11

But reports are not understanding.

A spreadsheet tells you what happened. It cannot tell you why a person hesitated. It cannot tell you what they were afraid of. It cannot tell you what they needed to feel before they could say yes.

That requires something different. Not more data. A different relationship with it.

05 / 11

The gap between those two is where growth is hiding.

On the other: businesses still guessing what their customers actually want.
